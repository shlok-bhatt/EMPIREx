EMPIREx · Action Cards

Action Cards

Action cards describe practical business decisions, such as Brand Advertisement. Every Action belongs to a business category.

What this card does

Action in gameplay

Each player receives three Action options from three different categories and secretly chooses one. All selected Actions are revealed together. The chosen category determines the scoring lookup and possible bonuses.
