Seven-round game guide

How to play.

Every round follows a clear sequence: reveal the situation, choose an Action, turn over the hidden Market Pulse and calculate the score.

Round scoring

max(0, C + E)
+ B + A

C = Customer score
E = Event modifier
B = Business bonus
A = Ability bonus

Scoring order matters

Apply the zero floor to C + E first. Then add Business and Ability bonuses.

01

Set up the players

Each player takes one Business card, one Ability card and one hidden Secret Goal. Keep Business and Ability visible; keep the Secret Goal private.

02

Reveal the round situation

Reveal one Customer card and one Event card for the round. Shuffle Market Pulse A, B and C, then draw one face down.

03

Offer three Actions

Give each player three Action options from three different categories. Each player secretly chooses one Action and places it face down.

04

Reveal the decisions

All players reveal their selected Actions together. Keep the Market Pulse hidden until every Action has been revealed.

05

Reveal the Market Pulse

Turn over the selected Pulse card. Use its matching Customer scoring profile to find the score for each player's chosen Action category.

06

Apply the Event and bonuses

Check the Event modifier for the chosen Action category. Then add +1 EP if it matches the player's Business speciality. An eligible Ability may add +2 EP once per game, if announced before scoring.

07

Calculate and reset

Calculate each player's Round EP. Discard the Customer and Event and played Actions. Return unchosen Action options to the Action deck and shuffle. Return all three Market Pulse cards and shuffle.

08

Complete seven rounds and finish

Repeat the round sequence for seven rounds. After Round 7, reveal Secret Goals. Each completed Goal adds +8 EP once. The player with the highest final EP wins; tied players share victory.

Scoring at a glance

What adds to your score?

Customer score · C

Look up the chosen Action category in the active Customer scoring profile. The score is 0, 1, 3 or 5.

Event modifier · E

Apply the Event modifier shown for the chosen Action category: +1, −1 or 0.

Business bonus · B

Add +1 EP when the chosen Action category matches the speciality on the Business card.

Ability bonus · A

Add +2 EP once per game when the chosen Action is in either eligible category. Announce before scoring and turn the Ability card sideways.

Perfect Match

A Customer score of exactly 5 before the Event modifier is a Perfect Match. It gives no extra EP by itself, but may count towards a relevant Secret Goal.
