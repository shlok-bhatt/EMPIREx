The complete seven-round guide

How to play.

Read the round in order: set up your cards, respond to the Customer and Event, choose an Action, reveal Market Pulse and calculate Enterprise Points.

Before Round 1

Set up your business.

Each player starts with three identity or objective cards. Know which cards stay visible and which one stays private.

01

Business card

Take one Business card. Keep it visible and note its named speciality, which can earn +1 EP when your selected Action category matches.

02

Ability card

Take one Ability card and keep it visible. Check its two eligible Action categories. Its +2 EP advantage may be used once per game and must be announced before scoring.

03

Secret Goal

Take one Secret Goal and keep it hidden. After Round 7, reveal it; a completed condition adds +8 EP once.

Repeat this sequence for seven rounds

One round, eight steps.

Keep the Market Pulse card face down until every player has revealed their selected Action. That reveal order is a central part of the game.

Round scoring formula

max(0, C + E)
+ B + A

C
Customer score
E
Event modifier
B
Business bonus (+1 EP when matched)
A
Ability bonus (+2 EP when eligible and used)
Scoring order matters

First calculate C + E and apply the zero floor. Only then add the Business and Ability bonuses.

EP means Enterprise Points.

01

ROUND REVEAL

Reveal the situation

Reveal one Customer card and one Event card for the round. Shuffle Market Pulse A, B and C, then draw one Pulse card face down.

02

ACTION OPTIONS

Offer three Actions

Give each player three Action options from three different categories. Each player secretly chooses one Action and places it face down.

03

SIMULTANEOUS REVEAL

Reveal the decisions together

All players reveal their selected Actions together. Keep the Market Pulse card hidden until every Action has been revealed.

04

PROFILE SELECTION

Reveal Market Pulse

Turn over the selected Pulse card. Use its matching Customer scoring profile to find the score for each player's chosen Action category.

05

EVENT MODIFIER

Apply the Event

Check the Event modifier for each player's chosen Action category. The modifier is +1, −1 or 0 EP.

06

PLAYER BONUSES

Add Business and Ability bonuses

Add +1 EP if the selected Action category matches the player's Business speciality. An eligible Ability may add +2 EP once per game, if announced before scoring; turn the Ability card sideways when used.

07

ROUND SCORE AND RESET

Calculate and reset

Calculate each player's Round EP using the formula. Discard the Customer and Event and played Actions. Return unchosen Action options to the Action deck and shuffle. Return all three Market Pulse cards and shuffle.

08

NEXT ROUND

Move to the next round

Repeat the same sequence until all seven rounds are complete. Keep track of each player's accumulated Enterprise Points.

Understand the formula

How each part
adds up.

The selected Action category connects the Customer score, Event modifier and any player-specific bonus.

C

Customer score

Use the active Customer scoring profile to find the score for the selected Action category: 0, 1, 3 or 5 EP.

E

Event modifier

Apply the modifier shown for the selected category: +1, −1 or 0 EP. This is combined with C before the zero floor.

B

Business bonus

Add +1 EP when the chosen Action category matches the speciality on the player's Business card.

A

Ability bonus

Add +2 EP once per game when the selected Action belongs to either eligible category. Announce before scoring and turn the card sideways.

Perfect Match

A Customer score of exactly 5 before the Event modifier is a Perfect Match. It gives no extra EP by itself, but may count towards a relevant Secret Goal.

Illustrative scoring example max(0, 3 − 1) + 1 + 0 = 3 EP

Customer score 3, Event modifier −1, Business bonus +1 and no Ability bonus. The zero floor is applied before adding the Business and Ability bonuses.

Need the profile rule?

Market Pulse chooses the scoring profile.

Its A, B or C card stays hidden until all Actions are revealed.

After Round 7

Reveal your Secret Goal.

Each player reveals their Secret Goal. If its stated condition has been completed, add +8 EP once to that player's total.

FINAL RESULT Highest total EP wins.

The player with the highest final Enterprise Points wins. If players are tied, they share victory.
