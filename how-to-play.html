How to Play EMPIREx

Complete seven-round walkthrough: setup, reveal, choose, turn, score and end-game.

Round scoring

max(0, C + E) + B + A

C = Customer score · E = Event modifier · B = Business bonus · A = Ability bonus.

Important

Apply the zero floor to C + E before adding B and A. Perfect Match means C is exactly 5 before the Event modifier; it gives no extra EP itself but counts towards relevant Secret Goals.

01

Setup

Each player takes one Business card, one Ability card and one hidden Secret Goal. Keep Business and Ability visible; keep the Secret Goal private.

02

Reveal

Reveal one Customer and one Event for everyone. Shuffle Market Pulse A, B and C, then draw one face down.

03

Deal and choose

Give each player three Action options from three different categories. Each player secretly chooses one Action and places it face down. Choices may be simultaneous.

04

Turn

All players reveal their chosen Actions at the same time. Only after Actions are locked and revealed, turn over the Market Pulse card.

05

Look up

Use the revealed Market Pulse profile to find the Customer score for the chosen Action category. Then check the Event reference for the modifier.

06

Score

Calculate Round EP. Add the Business bonus if the category matches the Business speciality. A player may use the Ability bonus once per game if eligible; announce it before scoring.

07

Reset

Discard the Customer and Event. Put chosen Actions in the played pile. Return unchosen Action options to the Action deck and shuffle. Return all three Market Pulse cards and shuffle.

08

End of Round 7

Reveal Secret Goals. Each completed Goal adds +8 EP once. The player with the highest final EP wins; tied players share victory.

Customer score

0, 1, 3 or 5 according to the active Customer profile and chosen Action category.

Event modifier

+1 for the listed positive category, −1 for the listed negative category and 0 for all others.

Business bonus

+1 EP when the chosen Action category matches your Business speciality.

Ability bonus

+2 EP once per game if the chosen Action is in either eligible category. Announce before scoring and turn the card sideways.

Worked scoring example

max(0, 3 − 1) + 1 + 0 = 3 EP

Example only: C = 3, E = −1, Business bonus = +1, Ability bonus = 0.
