Customer Cards

Customer cards introduce the customer group for the round. Each profile scores how well the chosen Action category fits that group.

What this card does

Customer in gameplay

After the Market Pulse is revealed, look up the chosen Action category in the active Customer profile. Scores are 0, 1, 3 or 5. A score of 5 is a Perfect Match before the Event modifier.
