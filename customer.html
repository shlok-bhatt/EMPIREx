Role in the game

Customer in play.

The Customer card sets the customer group for the round. The active Customer scoring profile shows how well each Action category fits that group. Customer scores are 0, 1, 3 or 5 EP.

A score of exactly 5 before the Event modifier is a Perfect Match. It does not give extra EP by itself, but it may count towards a relevant Secret Goal.
