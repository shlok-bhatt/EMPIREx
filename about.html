The idea behind the game

Our story.

A student experience became a question: how could more learners get the chance to practise enterprise?

Where EMPIREx began

We experienced practical enterprise learning.
We wanted others to experience it too.

As students, we had the opportunity to learn enterprise practically through the IGCSE curriculum. It allowed us to move beyond memorising concepts: we could explore ideas, make decisions, apply our learning and showcase our skills.

But we realised that not every student gets the same opportunity to practise enterprise in a practical and engaging way. Many learners may study business concepts without getting regular chances to test their judgement in realistic situations.

That is where EMPIREx started. We set out to create a physical card game that turns enterprise ideas into decisions. Players consider customer needs, choose business actions, respond to market events and see how their choices affect their Enterprise Points.

EMPIREx is designed as a supplementary learning experience. It does not replace teachers, lessons or the curriculum; it gives students another way to apply what they learn.
