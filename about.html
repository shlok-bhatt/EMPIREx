Our Story

What if students did not just learn entrepreneurship — what if they had to practise it?

EMPIREx was developed as a student-led enterprise project to make practical business decision-making more interactive through a physical card game.

It gives players a customer, a changing market, possible business actions and a reason to think carefully before choosing. EMPIREx supports application and discussion; it does not replace classroom teaching.
