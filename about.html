The idea behind the game

Our story.

A practical learning experience led to a simple question: how could more students get opportunities to practise enterprise decisions, not just read about them?

Student-led project Physical card game Practical decision-making

Where EMPIREx began

From learning enterprise
to practising decisions.

As students, we experienced practical enterprise learning through our studies. It gave us opportunities to explore ideas, make decisions, apply concepts and present our thinking.

That experience made us think about learners who may study business and enterprise concepts but have fewer opportunities to practise decisions in realistic situations. We wanted to create an activity that could help make that practice more engaging and repeatable.

EMPIREx grew from that idea. We developed a physical card game in which players consider customer needs, select business Actions, respond to market Events and see how their decisions affect their Enterprise Points.

It is designed as a supplementary learning experience. It does not replace teachers, lessons or the curriculum; it gives learners another way to apply and discuss what they are learning.

What guides the product

Built around
active learning.

These principles shape how we think about the game and the experience we want it to offer.

01

Make a decision

Players should have to choose a response, not only recall a definition.

02

Understand the reason

Customer needs and market conditions give decisions a context.

03

Consider consequences

Scoring helps players compare outcomes and explain their thinking.

04

Learn through replay

New rounds create fresh situations in which players can reconsider their approach.
