EMPIREx · Card family

Ability cards.

Use a special capability at the right moment. An Ability card gives a player a special advantage linked to two eligible Action categories. The player chooses when to use it.

Role in the game

Ability in play.

The bonus can be used once per game. When the chosen Action is in either eligible category, announce it before scoring, add +2 EP and turn the Ability card sideways.
