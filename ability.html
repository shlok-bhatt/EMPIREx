Ability Cards

An Ability card gives a player an advantage linked to two eligible Action categories. Branding Expert is one example.

What this card does

Ability in gameplay

Once per game, if the chosen Action is in either eligible category, announce the bonus before scoring, add +2 EP and turn the Ability card sideways.
