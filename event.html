Event cards.

Respond to changing market conditions. An Event card introduces a market situation that may affect the outcome of a business decision. Its modifier depends on the category of Action selected.

Role in the game

Event in play.

The Inflation example shown in the card sheet gives +1 EP to Finance, −1 EP to Sales & Promotions and no change to all other Action categories. Always use the modifier printed for the Event in play.
