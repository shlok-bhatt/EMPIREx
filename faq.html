Helpful information

Frequently asked questions.

A quick guide to the game, classroom use, pricing and enquiries.

What is EMPIREx?

EMPIREx is a physical enterprise card game. Players practise business decisions using Customer, Action, Event, Ability, Secret Goal and Business cards, alongside Market Pulse profiles and Enterprise Points scoring.

Who is it designed for?

It is designed for secondary-school learners, with an intended age range of approximately 13–17.

How many rounds are played?

A standard game follows seven rounds. Secret Goals are revealed and scored after Round 7.

How does a round work?

Reveal a Customer and Event, draw a hidden Market Pulse, give each player three Action options from different categories, choose one secretly, reveal Actions together, reveal the Pulse and calculate scores.

How is the score calculated?

Round EP = max(0, Customer score + Event modifier) + Business bonus + Ability bonus. The zero floor applies to the Customer score plus Event modifier before bonuses are added.

Does EMPIREx replace classroom teaching?

No. It is designed as a supplementary activity that encourages application and discussion alongside existing teaching.
