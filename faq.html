01 / The product

Understanding the game.

What is EMPIREx?

EMPIREx is a physical enterprise card game. Players practise business decisions using Customer, Action, Event, Ability, Secret Goal and Business cards, alongside Market Pulse profiles and Enterprise Points scoring.

Who is it designed for?

It is designed for secondary-school learners, with an intended age range of approximately 13–17.

How many rounds are played?

A standard game follows seven rounds. Secret Goals are revealed and checked after Round 7.

How does a round work?

Reveal a Customer and Event, then draw one Market Pulse card face down. Give each player three Action options from different categories. Players secretly choose one, reveal their selected Actions together, and only then reveal Market Pulse to identify the Customer scoring profile.

How is the score calculated?

Round EP = max(0, Customer score + Event modifier) + Business bonus + Ability bonus. Apply the zero floor to the Customer score plus Event modifier first; add the Business and Ability bonuses afterwards.

What is a Perfect Match?

A Customer score of exactly 5 before the Event modifier is a Perfect Match. It does not award extra EP by itself, but it may count towards a relevant Secret Goal.

02 / Classroom use

Learning and schools.

Does EMPIREx replace classroom teaching?

No. It is designed as a supplementary activity that supports application and discussion alongside existing teaching. It does not replace teachers, lessons or the curriculum.

How can teachers use it?

Teachers can use the round situations and player decisions to prompt discussion about customer needs, business choices, market changes and the reasons behind different outcomes. Possible classroom uses are described on the For Schools page.

Can schools enquire about multiple decks?

Yes. Schools and institutions can contact the team with their approximate quantity and requirements. Current availability and any institutional pricing or quotation will be confirmed directly in writing.
