FAQs

Answers about gameplay, pricing, classroom use and enquiries.

What is EMPIREx?

A physical enterprise card game for practising business decisions through Customer profiles, Actions, Events, Market Pulse and EP scoring.

How many rounds are played?

The current EMPIREx 2.0 Quick Rules describe a seven-round game.

How does a round work?

Reveal Customer and Event, draw a hidden Pulse, deal three different-category Actions, choose one secretly, reveal Actions together, reveal Pulse and calculate scores.

How is the score calculated?

Round EP = max(0, Customer score + Event modifier) + Business bonus + Ability bonus.
