The next chapter of EMPIREx

Future Editions.
Coming soon.

The core game is only the beginning. We are exploring new ways for students to practise enterprise through themed card packs, Indian market editions, classroom experiences and digital simulation.

Future concepts under consideration

These are future concepts being explored for development. They are not currently available to purchase, and release dates have not been announced. Final editions will depend on testing, feedback and feasibility.
