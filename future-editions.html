Ideas being explored

Future concepts.

We are exploring possible ways to extend the EMPIREx learning experience through themed card packs, regional and subject editions, digital ideas and school activities.

Concepts only · Not currently available

Ideas, not
announced products.

Everything on this page is a concept under consideration. No future edition is currently available to purchase, no release dates have been announced, and this page is not a pre-order or reservation list. Any development would depend on testing, feedback, resources and feasibility.
