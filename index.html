The idea behind the game

Business is not only
what you know.

It is also what you decide to do with what you know. EMPIREx creates a structured setting in which players consider customer needs, select a business response, adapt to an event and discuss the result.

It is a physical, face-to-face activity designed to provide supplementary practice and conversation around enterprise decisions.

The game at a glance

Read the market.
Make the call.

Each round builds around a customer situation, an event, a hidden scoring-profile selector and one secret Action choice per player.

The thinking behind EMPIREx

Created from a
practical question.

As students, we experienced opportunities to apply Enterprise concepts through decisions, discussion and project work. We wanted to create another way for learners to practise business choices through a repeatable physical activity.

That idea became EMPIREx: a student-led card game built around customers, actions, changing market situations and consequences.
