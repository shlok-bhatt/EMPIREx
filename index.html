A round at a glance

Understand the situation.
Make the call.

Each round asks players to read the market, choose one Action from three options, reveal their decisions together and then calculate the result.

{% include home-round-steps.html %}

Before the first round: each player receives a Business card, an Ability card and a private Secret Goal. After Round 7, completed Secret Goals are revealed; each completed Goal adds +8 EP once.

Why we created EMPIREx

We had the opportunity.
We wanted to share it.

As students, we experienced practical Enterprise learning through the IGCSE curriculum. We could apply concepts, make decisions and discuss outcomes — not only study ideas in theory.

We wanted to create another way for students to practise business decisions through a physical, discussion-led game. That idea became EMPIREx.
