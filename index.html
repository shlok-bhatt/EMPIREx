A physical, decision-based card game designed for secondary-school learning.

The opportunity

Knowing business is one thing.
Practising it is another.

EMPIREx gives players repeated opportunities to think about customers, choose business actions and respond to market conditions.

It is designed as a supplementary learning activity — not a replacement for teaching, curriculum or teacher guidance.
