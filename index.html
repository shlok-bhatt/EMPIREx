The opportunity

Knowing business is one thing. Running it is another.

Students may study enterprise concepts, but meaningful opportunities to repeatedly practise realistic business decisions can be limited. EMPIREx is designed to make that practice active, social and memorable.

Players respond to customer profiles, market conditions and business opportunities. Every choice becomes part of a strategy — and every round brings a new situation to consider.

“Don't just learn the language of business. Make the decisions.”

The round

Play. Decide. Score.

The supplied Quick Rules card presents a four-stage round. Here is the flow in a clear format.

Round EP = max(0, C + E + B + A)Customer + Event + Business + Action modifiers
1

Reveal

Reveal a Customer and an Event. Place one Market Pulse face down.

2

Choose

Take three Actions from different categories. Choose one face down.

3

Turn

Reveal all Actions, then reveal the Market Pulse.

4

Score

Use the Customer Profile and Event modifier to calculate the round's Enterprise Points.

For educators and institutions

Make enterprise interactive.

EMPIREx can be used as a supplementary activity to encourage students to apply enterprise ideas, discuss decisions and reflect on outcomes.

Possible classroom use

Use the game as a group activity, an enrichment exercise or a discussion starter alongside existing enterprise learning. Teachers can invite students to explain why they chose an action and how a changing market situation affected their strategy.

EMPIREx is a supplementary learning activity. It is not a replacement for curriculum teaching, assessment or teacher guidance.

Questions

Frequently asked.

What is EMPIREx?

EMPIREx is a physical enterprise card game that gives players opportunities to practise business decisions through customer profiles, actions, events and scoring.

Who is the game designed for?

It is designed for secondary-school students and group learning. Please contact the team for guidance on suitability for a particular class or setting.

Does EMPIREx replace enterprise lessons?

No. It is intended as a supplementary activity that supports practical application and discussion alongside teaching.

How does scoring work?

The Quick Rules card shows a round score based on Customer, Event, Business and Action values: Round EP = max(0, C + E + B + A). The printed rules provide the detailed scoring instructions.

What is the price?

The investor showcase currently displays an MRP of ₹599 and an introductory selling price of ₹549. Institutional prices shown are proposed and require confirmation.
