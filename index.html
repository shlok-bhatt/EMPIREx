The opportunity

Knowing business is one thing.
Practising it is another.

Students may learn enterprise concepts in class, but meaningful opportunities to repeatedly practise realistic business decisions can be limited. EMPIREx is designed to make that practice active, social and memorable.

Players consider customer needs, respond to market conditions and choose business actions. The aim is not simply to know a definition — it is to think about what a business should do next.

“Don't just learn the language of business. Make the decisions.”

Inside the experience

Six card types.
One business challenge.

Each category brings a different element into the round. The distinct card colours make the deck easy to navigate during play.

01CUSTOMER

Know who you're serving.

Customer Profile cards introduce different customer groups and the needs a business must consider. EXAMPLE · SENIOR CITIZENS

02ACTION

Choose what to do.

Action cards represent business moves that players select as part of their strategy. EXAMPLE · BRAND ADVERTISEMENT

03EVENTS

Respond to change.

Event cards introduce market conditions that can affect business decisions. EXAMPLE · INFLATION

04ABILITY

Use your strengths.

Ability cards bring additional capabilities and effects into the game. EXAMPLE · BRANDING EXPERT

05SECRET GOAL

Work towards a target.

Secret Goal cards give players an additional objective to pursue. EXAMPLE · MARKETING MASTER

06BUSINESS

Build your approach.

Business cards establish a business identity and its area of specialisation. EXAMPLE · FASHION

The supplied Quick Rules card presents a four-stage round. This summary follows the sequence shown on that card.

ROUND ENTERPRISE POINTSmax(0, C + E + B + A)Customer + Event + Business + Action
01

Reveal

Reveal a Customer and an Event. Put one Market Pulse face down.

02

Choose

Take three Actions from different categories. Choose one face down.

03

Turn

Reveal all Actions, then reveal the Market Pulse.

04

Score

Use the Customer Profile and Event modifier to calculate the round score.

For complete gameplay instructions, card-specific effects and any clarifications, use the final printed rules supplied with the game.

What is EMPIREx?

EMPIREx is a physical enterprise card game designed to help students practise business decisions through customer profiles, actions, events and Enterprise Points scoring.

Who is it designed for?

It is designed for secondary-school learning and group play. Contact the team for guidance on using it in a particular class or setting.

Does it replace enterprise lessons?

No. EMPIREx is intended as a supplementary activity to support practical application and discussion alongside teaching.

How does a round work?

The Quick Rules show four stages: Reveal, Choose, Turn and Score. The round score uses Customer, Event, Business and Action values, with a minimum of zero EP.

What is the current price?

The investor showcase displays an MRP of ₹599 and an introductory selling price of ₹549. Proposed institutional prices are indicative and require confirmation.
