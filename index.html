Why we started

We had the opportunity.
We wanted to share it.

As students, we had the opportunity to learn enterprise practically through the IGCSE curriculum. We could apply concepts, make decisions and showcase our skills — not only study ideas in theory.

We realised that not every student gets the same opportunity to practise enterprise in an active, engaging way. That is where we started EMPIREx.

We created a physical card game that brings business decisions into students’ hands through customer profiles, business actions and changing market conditions.

The learning experience

More than knowing the terms.
Practise making choices.

Each round gives players a situation to understand, a decision to make and an outcome to evaluate.

01 / CUSTOMERS

Understand the customer

Consider which business actions best match the needs and preferences of the customer group in play.

02 / DECISIONS

Choose your move

Compare three different Action categories and choose one in response to the situation.

03 / ADAPTATION

Respond to change

React to market Events and an unknown Market Pulse profile before scoring the result.
