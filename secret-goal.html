Secret Goal cards.

Work towards a hidden objective. Each player receives one Secret Goal at the beginning of the game and keeps it private while playing. It creates an extra objective alongside the main score.

Role in the game

Secret Goal in play.

After Round 7, reveal the Secret Goal. If its stated condition is complete, add +8 EP once to the final total. The Marketing Master example requires Marketing Actions in at least two different rounds.
