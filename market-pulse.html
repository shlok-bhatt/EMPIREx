The hidden profile selector

Market Pulse.

Market Pulse A, B and C determine which Customer scoring profile is used for the round. The selected profile stays hidden until players have revealed their Actions.

A

Profile A

Selects Customer scoring profile A for the round.

B

Profile B

Selects Customer scoring profile B for the round.

C

Profile C

Selects Customer scoring profile C for the round.

When is it revealed?

Shuffle all three Pulse cards and draw one face down before players choose an Action. Reveal every player's Action first, then turn over the Pulse card and use the matching Customer scoring profile. After scoring, return all three Pulse cards and shuffle them.
