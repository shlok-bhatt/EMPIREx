The hidden profile selector

Market Pulse.

Market Pulse A, B and C determine which Customer scoring profile is used for a round. The selected card remains face down until every player has revealed their Action.

A Profile AB Profile BC Profile C

Three selector cards

One letter selects
one profile.

The Pulse card does not replace the Customer or Action. It tells players which Customer scoring profile to use when they look up the score for their selected Action category.

SELECTOR A

Profile A

Use Customer scoring profile A for this round.

SELECTOR B

Profile B

Use Customer scoring profile B for this round.

SELECTOR C

Profile C

Use Customer scoring profile C for this round.

The reveal order

Keep it hidden.
Reveal it last.

The selected Pulse card stays face down while players make and reveal their Action choices. Only then does the profile become known.

01
Shuffle A, B and C

Shuffle all three Market Pulse cards and draw one face down for the round.

02
Choose and reveal Actions

Each player chooses one Action secretly. All selected Actions are revealed together while the Pulse card stays hidden.

03
Turn over the Pulse card

Reveal the selected A, B or C card only after every Action has been revealed.

04
Use the matching Customer profile

Find the score for each selected Action category in the Customer scoring profile named by the Pulse card.

05
Reset for the next round

After scoring, return all three Market Pulse cards and shuffle them.

Remember

Market Pulse selects the Customer scoring profile. The Event modifier and Business or Ability bonuses are applied separately under the normal round-scoring formula.
