For teachers, schools and learning groups

Bring enterprise
into the room.

EMPIREx is a physical enterprise card game that gives secondary-school learners a structured way to practise business decisions, discuss their reasoning and consider the consequences of different choices.

13–17 intended age range 07 game rounds Physical card-based activity
Classroom use Learning focus School enquiries

A practical supplement

Use the game to
start a conversation.

EMPIREx is intended to sit alongside teaching, not replace lessons, teacher guidance or the curriculum. A teacher can use the game as a discussion activity after learners have explored relevant enterprise ideas.

01 / CONSIDER

Understand the situation

Look at the Customer and Event cards. Discuss the needs, constraints or market conditions that may affect a business.

02 / DECIDE

Choose an Action

Compare the available Action categories and explain why one decision may be more suitable than another.

03 / REVIEW

Discuss the outcome

Use the scoring result to consider how the customer profile, market event and business speciality affected the decision.

Possible discussion areas

Make the thinking
visible.

The game creates opportunities for learners to explain their choices. The teacher can guide discussion towards the enterprise skills being explored in class.

01

Decision-making

What information influenced the Action chosen? What alternatives were considered?

02

Customer understanding

How can different customer needs change which business response is appropriate?

03

Adaptability

How might a market Event change the outcome of the same Action category?

04

Reasoning and reflection

What would the player keep or change when making a similar decision in another round?
