Website information

Privacy notice.

A plain-language explanation of the information involved when you visit this website or choose to contact the EMPIREx team.

This notice describes the EMPIREx information website. The site is used to explain the physical card game and provide email contact links. It does not have user accounts, an enquiry form, online checkout or payment processing.

Information you choose to send

When you select an email link, your email application or registered email service opens with a message addressed to a team member. Opening the link does not send a message automatically. Any message is sent only if you choose to send it through your email service.

Information you include in an email is handled by your email provider and the recipient's email account. It is not submitted through an enquiry form on this website. Avoid including passwords, financial details or other sensitive information in a general enquiry.

Website hosting and technical requests

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. When a page is requested, the hosting provider may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the service under its own policies.

Fonts and external services

This website loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google's font service to request those files. Google and any email service you use operate under their own terms and privacy information.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The website's own code does not intentionally create visitor accounts, use analytics scripts or embed advertising trackers. The Shop demonstration does not store cart state or submit personal information. The Shop page is a non-transactional investor demonstration. Its cart uses temporary in-page memory only; its checkout fields are non-interactive examples. The site does not submit orders, collect customer details or process payments. Your browser and external services may have their own settings and technical processes.

Children and school enquiries

EMPIREx is intended for secondary-school learners. Students making an enquiry on behalf of a school should involve a teacher or appropriate school representative. Please share only the information needed to explain the enquiry.

External websites

Links to email services and other external websites are governed by the policies of those providers. Review their privacy information before using them.

Questions about this notice

For a question about this website notice, contact the team through the Contact page.
